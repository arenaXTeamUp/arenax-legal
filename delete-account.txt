ArenaX - Account and Data Deletion

Last updated: October 15, 2026

You can request deletion of your ArenaX account and associated data at any time.

HOW TO DELETE YOUR ACCOUNT

Option 1: From within the App
1. Open the ArenaX app and sign in.
2. Go to Profile > Settings.
3. Tap "Delete Account" and confirm.

Option 2: By email
1. Send an email to [YOUR_SUPPORT_EMAIL] from the email address registered to your account.
2. Use the subject line: "Account Deletion Request".
3. Include your username (and your registered email address) in the message.
4. We will confirm your request and complete the deletion within 30 days.

DATA THAT WILL BE DELETED
- Your account information (name, username, email address).
- Your profile information and profile picture.
- Your in-app activity and usage data linked to your account.

DATA THAT MAY BE RETAINED
- Records we are required to keep by law (for example, transaction or tax records), kept only for the legally required period.
- Anonymized or aggregated data that cannot identify you.
- Backup copies, which are automatically removed within 30 days.

IMPORTANT
Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Any progress, balances, or content associated with your account will be lost.

CONTACT US
For questions about account or data deletion, contact: support.arenax@gmail.com
